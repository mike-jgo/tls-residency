# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The LaSallian staffers tap an RFID card when arriving at or leaving the office. First-time staffers register at the shared office computer. Admins manage people and review residency hours on that computer or the office network.

## Product Purpose

Record attendance accurately on a Raspberry Pi, including while Google Sheets is unreachable. People must know whether a tap or roster change was saved and how to recover without accidentally changing attendance twice.

## Brand Personality

Plain, clear, dependable. Preserve the existing task-focused interface and The LaSallian identity.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, unnecessary steps, ambiguous success messages, and instructions that require technical knowledge to follow.

## Design Principles

- Make the next action explicit, especially after registration or an error.
- Never claim a write failed when its outcome is uncertain.
- Preserve work during recoverable failures and make retries safe.
- Returning to the shared tap screen locks administration again.
- Keep existing attendance and hours policies explicit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard operation, named form controls, clear focus, and screen-reader status announcements. Communicate status with words as well as color. Keep the layout usable on office monitors and mobile admin screens.
